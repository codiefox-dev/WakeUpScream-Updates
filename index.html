How to Install

Since WakeUpScream is distributed directly (not through the Play Store), you will need to bypass the standard Play Protect warning.

1

Download the APK

Click the download button above to download the app-release.apk file.

2

Open the File

Open the downloaded file from your notification bar or file manager. You may see a warning saying "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."

3

Allow Unknown Sources

Tap Settings on the popup, and enable "Allow from this source". Then click Back and tap Install.

4

Bypass Play Protect (If shown)

If Google Play Protect says "Unsafe app blocked" or "App scan recommended", tap "More details" and then tap "Install Anyway". (This happens because the app is new and not yet reviewed by Google).

5

Grant Permissions

Open the app, enter your name, and grant the Notification Permission. For the best experience, go to Settings inside the app and enable "Ignore Battery Optimization".
